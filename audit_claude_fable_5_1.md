**Assessment of the TWAP fairness justification (DESIGN.md §16.3).** Accepted with caveats.
- A same-block front-run cannot move the reference: the average is computed from the pair's own
  cumulative counters between a checkpoint ≥ 30 minutes old and now (`PancakeV2Twap.consult`,
  `StonksPadSwapLib._pickObservation`). Moving a 30-minute average by the 5% default band requires
  holding a ≥ 5% displacement for the whole window against arbitrage; with ≈ 125 BNB of quote-side
  liquidity in QQQB/STONKS that is several BNB of inventory at risk for ≤ 5% of a ≤ 20 BNB (default
  5 BNB, recommended ≤ 0.5 BNB) buy. The economics are unfavourable to the attacker at current sizes.
- Checkpoint integrity: `updateTwapObservations` is permissionless but only *when* to record is
  chosen; the recorded value is the pair's cumulative. `_recordObservation` refuses to overwrite a
  `latest` younger than 30 minutes, so a refresh cannot shrink the window below the minimum.
- Keeper window choice (residual, M-02 b): the keeper picks between two checkpoints indirectly (by
  timing the record and the buy) and could prefer the lower of the two averages in a falling market.
  The extra headroom is bounded by real recent price history, not by a manipulable spot, and by the
  deviation cap. Acceptable; documented.
- Arithmetic: cumulative differences and `uint32` timestamps are `unchecked` on purpose and wrap
  consistently with the pair (UniswapV2 convention); `Math.mulDiv` avoids overflow in `consult`;
  `elapsed == 0` is impossible because the window check requires ≥ 30 minutes. A checkpoint whose
  `uint32` timestamp is exactly 0 would read as "missing" — a 2106 edge, noted (I-04).
- Fee-awareness: the reference is net of the 0.25% V2 fee per hop, so the band is spent only on
  impact / manipulation. Reasonable and tested (`test_buyStocks_TwapFloorBlocksManipulatedSpot`:
  a 40 BNB pump immediately before the keeper's buy is rejected with `minOut below oracle floor`).

## Detailed Findings

### Medium

#### M-01: Commission structure deviates from Flap's recommendation (Rule 002 — justification required)
**Severity**: Medium · **Status**: Open (process) · **File**: `src/StonksPadVaultFactory.sol` (constructor, `platformFeeBps = 1000`), `src/StonksPadVault.sol` (`receive()`)
**Description**: Flat 10% of gross vault revenue; Flap recommends 6% for tax ≤ 1% and `6/taxRateBps` above.
**Recommendation**: Provide the written justification to Flap at audit (fee funds the STONKS buy & burn and NFT holders; hard cap 10%; snapshot per vault so it cannot be raised retroactively).

#### M-02: Reference-price inputs are admin-registered and only structurally validated; keeper can time TWAP checkpoints
**Severity**: Medium · **Status**: Open (accepted residual) · **File**: `src/StonksPadVaultFactory.sol` `registerStock`, `setBnbUsdFeed`, `updateTwapObservations`; `src/StonksPadSwapLib.sol` `_pickObservation`
**Description**: (a) `registerStock` checks only that a Chainlink feed answers `decimals()` and that TWAP hops are existing V2 pairs; an admin could register a wrong feed or a thin intermediate pair. (b) The keeper influences which of the two TWAP checkpoints is used by timing records and buys (bounded by the [30 min, 24 h] window and the deviation cap). Both need StonksPad-controlled keys; neither is unbounded (spend ≤ 20 BNB / 10 min).
**Recommendation**: Guardian-approved allowlist or timelock for feed / path registration; consider a longer minimum window (60 min) for TWAP-mode stocks; keep `maxSpendPerBuy` small relative to pool liquidity (DESIGN.md §16.3 guidance).

#### M-03: Exotic stock-token behaviours are not handled
**Severity**: Medium · **Status**: Open · **File**: `src/StonksPadVault.sol` `claimStocks`, `src/StonksPadSwapLib.sol`
**Description**: Blocklists / pauses on tokenized stocks would make `claimStocks` or `buyStocks` revert for that stock; rebases or airdropped surplus tokens are not accounted (surplus is not recoverable — no sweep by design).
**Recommendation**: Register only plain ERC-20 stocks; a future beacon upgrade can add a Guardian-gated surplus sweep if ever needed.

### Low

#### L-01: `StonksPadTreasury.distribute()` couples the two wallet payouts
**File**: `src/StonksPadTreasury.sol` `distribute()` — if one wallet rejects BNB, both payouts revert until `setWallets` is corrected. Admin-recoverable. Recommendation: pay each wallet independently or use pull-claims.

#### L-02: A disabled or mis-set mandatory stock blocks all launches
**File**: `src/StonksPadVaultFactory.sol` `newVault` (`Mandatory stock missing`, `Stock not registered`) — if `mandatoryStock` is later disabled, every launch reverts until admin re-enables or clears it. Admin-controlled, reversible; existing vaults unaffected (disabled stocks are skipped in `buyStocks`).

#### L-03: TWAP availability pause after a checkpoint gap
**File**: `src/StonksPadSwapLib.sol` `_pickObservation` — if no checkpoint is recorded for > 24 h, buys of TWAP-mode stocks pause until a new checkpoint is 30 minutes old. Safe direction; keeper should record hourly (documented).

#### L-04: Thin liquidity consumes the deviation band operationally
**File**: `src/StonksPadSwapLib.sol` `buyOne` — successive buys 10 minutes apart on the ≈ 70 BNB/hop STONKS route each move spot ≈ 0.5–1%, and the DEX quote already includes the previous impact; with a 5% band, spends above ≈ 0.5 BNB start to fail. Operational guidance in DESIGN.md / DEPLOY.md (0.2–0.5 BNB); admin may widen the band to ≤ 10%.

#### L-05: Relaxed validation accepts a very small vault share
**File**: `src/StonksPadVaultFactory.sol` `_validateBeforeLaunch` — `mktBps > 0` allows launches that route almost all tax to Flap-native deflation / LP; the vault then accrues little. Product decision; informational for launchers (policy `mktBps gt 0` is exposed to the UI).

### Informational

- **I-01** Privileged methods are omitted from `vaultUISchema()` by design (keeper/admin/Guardian only).
- **I-02** `StonksPadTreasury` is outside the Flap vault spec; it holds platform commission only, has no owner, is not upgradeable and exposes no emergency withdraw. Donations sent directly to it are split like commission.
- **I-03** `collect()` iterates a caller-supplied array (caller pays gas; only `isVault` addresses accepted).
- **I-04** `PancakeV2Twap` uses `uint32` timestamps (UniswapV2 convention); a checkpoint recorded at a `uint32` value of exactly 0 (year 2106) would read as missing.

### Residual risks (accepted)

- **Extraction bound = day cap.** A hostile keeper can collect up to `opsMaxPerDay` per vault per
  day (hard max 0.1 BNB, recommended 0.005 BNB) via unthrottled `publishDistribution` calls with
  dust amounts. It cannot exceed it, cannot touch fee routes / commission / bought stocks, every
  payout is an event, and the admin or Guardian can zero the caps.
- **Both keys compromised** (keeper + an already-active verifier): claims open after 30 minutes;
  the only remaining control is `cancelEpoch` inside that window, and the damage is bounded by the
  epoch's reserves. This is the explicit trade-off of the feature; the verifier must run on
  infrastructure separate from the keeper and from the admin key.
- **Guardian is not reimbursed**: the Flap Guardian contract rejects plain BNB; the payout fails
  silently and the action succeeds.
- **Verifier rotation suspends the fast path for 24h.**

**Fairness analysis of "new vaults don't wait 24h".** The 24h activation lock (finding M1) exists so
that whoever controls the admin key cannot introduce a verifier and use it inside the veto window.
The lock is tied to the *introduction of the key*, not to the vault's age: the vault copies
`defaultVerifierSince`. Cases: (a) default in place ≥ 24h → active at launch — no new key appeared,
and `DefaultVerifierUpdated` gave 24h of notice; (b) default changed < 24h ago → inert in new vaults
until factory time + 24h (`test_factoryDefaults_FreshDefaultVerifierIsInertInNewVaults`, boundary
checked at −1 s); (c) factory default changed → existing vaults untouched (copy semantics;
`…ExistingVaultsKeepTheirCopyAndOverridesStillWork`); (d) per-vault `setVerifier` → lock restarts in
that vault, replaced key loses power at once; (e) keeper rotated onto the default verifier → refused
at approval time. A launch can neither restart nor skip the lock, and the vault takes the timestamp
only from its own factory, whose setter always stamps `block.timestamp`.

Residuals: verifier key rotation is O(number of vaults) admin transactions (deliberate: no single
transaction can change the verifier of every live vault); the default caps apply to every future
launch, so a mis-set default propagates until corrected (bounded by the hard maxima; per-vault
`setOpsCaps` fixes existing ones); the vault trusts its factory for `verifierSince` (the factory is
immutable, non-upgradeable code from this repository).
